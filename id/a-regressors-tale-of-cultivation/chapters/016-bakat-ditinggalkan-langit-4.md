## Chapter Notes

- **New canonical terms:** Mountain Lord Martial Arts, Transcending Peaks Step, Gang Qi, Jiangshi, Heavenly Lake, Severing Mountain (secret technique), Blue/Red lines as Pinnacle Realm perception.
- **Continuity notes:** Tahun ke-45, Seo mampu mengolah kelima indera dan menghadapi peak master selama 10–20 jurus; ia masih belum mencapai Pinnacle Realm. Kim mengembangkan bola Gang Qi setelah melihat realm di atas Five Energies Converging to the Origin, tetapi meninggal setelah melarikan diri dari cultivator Core Formation. Seo menolak meninggalkan jasad gurunya, bertarung melawan Jiangshi selama tiga hari, lalu membunuh jasad itu dengan teknik rahasia Severing Mountain dan mencapai Pinnacle Realm. Setelah itu berakhir regresi keempat.
- **Ambiguities retained:** Source uses “peak”/“Pinnacle Realm” variably for martial realms; the chapter's distinctions are retained. The five spheres above Kim are described visually and linked by him to surpassing Five Energies, without naming a new canonical realm. The red and blue lines are presented as perception of the next attack and optimal path, respectively.
